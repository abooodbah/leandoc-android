First release. A read-only viewer for Word documents (.docx, .dotx, .docm, .dotm) that opens files from a file manager, email or browser. It uses the same reader as the desktop LeanDoc. Each document is cleaned first: macros, ActiveX, OLE objects, external links, remote templates and images, embedded fonts and comments are removed, and tracked changes are accepted. Old .doc files get a message and can be sent to another app. No permissions and no network.
